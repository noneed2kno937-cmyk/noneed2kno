Get a track from the hard drive to Spotify without fucking up the upload.

Real talk: Spotify doesn't let anybody upload straight to it anymore. Every independent track goes through a distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, etc). This app checks your files, builds your metadata, sends you to your distributor, then finds the release once it's live on Spotify and drops it on your site.
